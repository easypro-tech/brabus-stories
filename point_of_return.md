# Point of Return

He never promised he would be around.

The evening they met, she asked:

- Do you leave often?

- Almost always.

- Then why do you want a relationship at all?

He was silent for a long time.

- So there'd be somewhere to come back to.

She didn't answer. She just took his hand.

---

Before her, he'd had three wives. Each one still picks up when he calls.

She was the fourth.

The first he started thinking about staying with. Only it never worked out.

---

They didn't live together. He came. Or she came to him.

With her, everything was simple. Bag by the door. Jacket on the hook. Watch on the shelf.

He didn't say "I'm home." She heard the lock click and put the kettle on.

---

Work found him even without a phone.

At night. At a holiday table. In the middle of a film.

He always said it the same way:

- I have to go.

She helped him zip his jacket.

- Come back.

He came back.

From sand. From snow. From jungle. From places that aren't on any map.

Sometimes ten kilos lighter. Sometimes with a new scar. Sometimes he was silent for a week.

She put the kettle on. He took off his watch, put it on the shelf.

The house came together again.

---

They fought. Loudly. For real.

He could slam the door. She could cry.

Then one of them would say it first:

- We're fools.

And life went on.

Once he said he would be back by Sunday. He called on Wednesday. She asked if he had eaten.

He remembered only later that on Sunday they were going to her mother's. She had asked him to keep that day free a month ahead.

The next time he said he would come by the weekend, she answered:

- You'll come when you come.

He was glad, then, that she wasn't offended anymore.

---

Two years passed like that.

And then a gap appeared. For the first time in a long time, he knew that next Friday he would be where he was this Friday.

He wanted to tell her about it at home.

While he was gone, she cleared his things out of the closet.

By then he already knew who had been staying the night with her. On the way he kept trying to remember when she had last said "Come back."

He remembered the jacket. He remembered her hand on the zipper.

He didn't remember the words.

---

He arrived as usual.

Bag by the door. Watch - on the shelf.

He wanted to say "I'm home."

He didn't.

In the kitchen, everything was in its place. His cup was by the sugar bowl. Her sweater hung on the back of the chair.

He put his palm on the kettle.

The kettle was cold.

In the hallway he took the bag. Already on the stairs, he remembered the watch.

He came back for it.
